Welcome to holding page for Musica11y

This is the holding web page for the accessible musical WebXR application Musica11y.net. Musica11y is being developed as a portal for creating a customisable, accessible virtual reality musical instrument that can be accessed on 2D platforms (desktop and tablet formats) and in virtual reality (VR) and mixed reality (MR) or a mixture of the two (XR).

Musica11y.net seeks to employ diverse interaction methods to facilitate diverse access needs. It will be a programmable playground with an open-source approach to facilitating "a11y allies" who can contribute to the development of back-end features alongside a platform for sharing virtual reality musical instruments with peers.

This prototype WebXR application is supported by Future Screen's Northern Ireland's Proof of Concept Award.

This is work in progress

For tablets and desktop browsers select 2D Play.

To launch Musica11y in VR select 3D Play.

More Information

The release date of the prototype is planned for the start of third quarter 2025.
