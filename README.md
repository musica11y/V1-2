Musica11y.net is being developed as a portal for creating a customisable, accessible virtual reality musical instrument that can be accessed on 2D platforms (desktop and tablet formats) and in virtual reality (VR) and mixed reality (MR) or a mixture of the two (XR).

Musica11y.net seeks to employ diverse interaction methods to facilitate diverse access needs. It will be a programmable playground with an open-source approach to facilitating "a11y allies" who can contribute to the development of back-end features alongside a platform for sharing virtual reality musical instruments with peers.

This prototype WebXR application is supported by Future Screen's Northern Ireland's Proof of Concept Award.
